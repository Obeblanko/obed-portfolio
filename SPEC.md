# Portfolio Website Specification: Obed Blankson

## 1. Purpose
Get interviews and internship or junior-role offers in data analysis and
front-end development, and give the IT Hub early credibility with partners
and clients. The portfolio should also be living proof of my skills that
grows as I complete the milestones in my development plan.

## 2. Target audience
- Primary: recruiters and hiring managers for data analyst and front-end
  roles. They skim, so the headline must land in 10 seconds.
- Secondary: potential practitioner partners and mentors for the IT Hub.
- Secondary: young startup founders, schools and students, as future Hub
  clients or learners.

## 3. Personal positioning
IT graduate who combines data, front-end development and entrepreneurship.

Draft tagline for the Hero section:
"IT graduate who turns data into decisions and ideas into clean interfaces,
building toward a youth tech hub in Ghana."

What sets me apart:
- Four internships across software development, IT support and public-sector ICT
- A rare mix of data, front-end, UI/UX and entrepreneurship
- Projects aimed at real local problems (farmer markets, crop disease, air quality)
- A teaching background (Alpha Star Academy), which fits the Hub's training mission

## 4. Required sections (single page, sticky top nav)
1. **Hero**: name, tagline, photo, buttons for "View Projects", "Download CV"
   and "Contact".
2. **About**: short story covering teaching, IT, data and the Hub vision.
3. **Projects**: one card per project.
4. **Skills**: grouped as Data, Front-end, Programming, Cloud, Tools.
5. **Experience**: timeline of the four internships and teaching.
6. **Education and certifications**: TTU, Thrive Africa UI/UX, IBM,
   AWS re/Start, DigiVibe.
7. **IT Hub**: a short "what I'm building" section with a call for partners.
8. **Contact**: email form or mailto, LinkedIn, GitHub.

## 6. Functional requirements
- Project cards linking to the live demo and GitHub
- Downloadable CV
- Working contact option (a form service like Formspree, or mailto)
- Smooth-scroll navigation, mobile responsive
- Social links (GitHub, LinkedIn)
- Basic SEO: page title, meta description, social preview image

## 7. Design / UX
- Look: clean, modern, minimal, with generous spacing, showing off my UI/UX learning.
- Colours: one accent colour (for example deep teal, or Ghana-inspired gold
  on a neutral background). Optional dark mode.
- Layout: single-page scroll with a sticky top nav.
- Typography: one clean sans-serif font such as Inter or Poppins.
- Feel: professional but approachable. Collect 2 or 3 portfolios I like as references.
- Must work on phone and laptop.

## 8. Tech
Build with **React + Tailwind (Vite)** and deploy on Vercel or Netlify. This
matches my development plan and counts toward my React milestone. Since I am
still learning React, ask the AI to explain each component it generates so I
can maintain the site myself.
